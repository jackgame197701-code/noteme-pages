NoteMe 隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日

NoteMe（以下稱「本 App」）是由 JMMZ Design 開發的日曆筆記應用程式。本 App 採完全離線設計，我們非常重視您的隱私。

1. 資料收集

本 App不會收集、傳輸、分享或出售任何個人資料。您的所有筆記內容（包含標題、手繪、文字、照片、標籤）僅儲存於您裝置本機（IndexedDB），不會上傳至任何伺服器。

2. 裝置權限使用

  • 相機：僅用於拍攝照片並插入目前編輯中的筆記。拍攝的影像只儲存在本機，不會上傳。
  • 照片圖庫：僅用於讓您挑選照片插入筆記。所選照片只儲存在本機，不會上傳。

上述權限皆可在 iOS「設定」中隨時撤回，撤回後僅無法使用對應功能，不影響其他功能。

3. 第三方服務、廣告與追蹤

  • 本 App 未整合任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。
  • 本 App 不使用廣告識別碼（IDFA），不會跨 App 追蹤您。

4. 兒童隱私

本 App 適合所有年齡使用，且因不收集任何資料，不存在兒童資料收集問題。

5. 資料刪除

由於所有資料僅存於本機，您可隨時在 App 內刪除單篇筆記；解除安裝本 App 即會清除裝置上的全部資料。

6. 政策變更與聯絡

本政策如有變更，將更新於本頁並修正生效日期。如有任何隱私相關疑問，請聯絡：jackgame197701@gmail.com

NoteMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

NoteMe (the "App") is a calendar-based note-taking app developed by JMMZ Design. The App is designed to work fully offline, and we take your privacy seriously.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. All of your content (titles, drawings, text, photos, tags) is stored only on your device (local IndexedDB) and is never uploaded to any server.

2. Device Permissions

  • Camera: used only to take photos and insert them into the note you are editing. Photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Photo Library: used only to let you pick photos to insert into notes. Selected photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

You may revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings; only the corresponding feature will be unavailable.

3. Third Parties, Ads & Tracking

  • The App integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages, and since no data is collected, there is no children's data collection concern.

5. Data Deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you may delete individual notes at any time; uninstalling the App removes all of its on-device data.

6. Changes & Contact

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions, contact: jackgame197701@gmail.com
